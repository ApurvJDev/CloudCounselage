Data Deletion Request Instructions

If you wish to have your data deleted from the Cloud Counselage application, please follow these instructions. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your data is handled in accordance with your preferences.

Email Request: Send an email to apurvjadhav34@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request."

Include Necessary Information: In your email, please provide the following information to help us identify your account:

Your full name
The email address associated with your Cloud Counselage account
Your Google account ID (if applicable)
Specify Deletion Request: Clearly state that you want to delete your data from the Cloud Counselage application. You can include any additional details or preferences regarding the deletion process.

Identity Verification: To protect your data and privacy, we may require you to verify your identity. This could involve confirming your identity through your registered email address or other means to ensure that we are processing the request from the actual account owner.

Response Time: We will acknowledge your request within a reasonable time frame, typically within 30 days, and inform you once your data has been deleted. Please note that certain data may be retained for legal or security reasons.

By following these instructions, you can request the deletion of your data from the Cloud Counselage application. We take data privacy seriously and will process your request promptly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please feel free to contact us using the provided contact information in the Privacy Policy.
